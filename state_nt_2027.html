Northern Territory Redistribution 2027

Context

Northern Territory is divided into electoral districts, which must be drawn within 20% of the average district enrolment of (ie. between and ).

The Redistribution Commission may also consider projected district enrolments, with an aim for districts to also be within % of the average projected district enrolment of in 2030 (ie. between and ).

The ABS divides Northern Territory into 70 named Statistical Areas Level 2 (SA2s) which are divided into 649 Statistical Areas Level 1 (SA1s). These are the building blocks used to create electoral districts.

Some SA1s are actually split between multiple districts, however for the purposes of this tool SA1s cannot be split and have been fully placed in the most logical single district. As a result, some districts will have enrolment numbers that slightly differ from the offical figures.
