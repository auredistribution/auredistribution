Context

Queensland is divided into federal electoral districts, which must be drawn within 10% of the average district enrolment of (ie. between and ).

The Redistribution Commission will also consider projected district enrolments, with an aim for districts to also be within 3.5% of the average projected district enrolment of in 2032 (ie. between and ).

The ABS divides Queensland into 546 named Statistical Areas Level 2 (SA2s) which are divided into 12,545 Statistical Areas Level 1 (SA1s). These are the building blocks used to create electoral districts.

Some SA1s are split between multiple districts. This dataset includes split areas where available; each loaded SA1 code is counted once, even when its geometry has multiple pieces. Enrolment numbers may differ slightly from official figures.
